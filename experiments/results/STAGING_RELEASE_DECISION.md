  1. `"Normal Operation"`
  2. `"GPU Surge"`
  3. `"Cooling Degradation"`
  4. `"Thermal Hotspot"`
  5. `"High AI Workload"`
- **Authoritative Oracle Definition:** Analytical reduced-order thermal simulator oracle (`simulate_placement`) evaluating all hardware-feasible candidate racks and selecting the rack resulting in the lowest actual post-placement equilibrium temperature.
- **Background & Workload Protocol:** For each decision step, 10 consecutive random background workloads (500–2,000 W) are injected with 4 relaxation thermal convergence iterations; the evaluated test workload is AI Training with GPU demand drawn from $\{20.0\%, 40.0\%, 60.0\%\}$, memory demand fixed at $30.0\%$, and intensity level 3 (power estimate 2,000–3,000 W).
- **Thermal Safety Thresholds:** Critical safety threshold $T_{critical} = 75.0^\circ\text{C}$ (`config.TEMP_CRITICAL`), maximum safe operating threshold $T_{max\_safe} = 80.0^\circ\text{C}$ (`config.TEMP_MAX_SAFE`).
- **Candidate Filtering Rule:** Strict hardware feasibility ($GPU_{avail} \ge GPU_{demand} / 4$ and $MEM_{avail} \ge MEM_{demand} / 4$) and surrogate thermal safety ($T_{pred} < 75.0^\circ\text{C}$); if all candidates exceed $75.0^\circ\text{C}$, the fallback penalty formula is applied: $\text{Penalty} = 45.0 + 5.0 \times (T_{pred} - 75.0)$.
